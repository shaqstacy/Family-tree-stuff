# Source archive

Everything gathered for the Stacy Family Tree, kept so the facts on the site can be traced.

- `family-papers/` – the original scanned family documents (Zoglman and Stallings tree pages, the
  "Stallings Family History" memoir, and Theresa Zoglman's 2009 eulogy)
- `obituaries/` – full obituary texts, from funeral-home pages or transcribed from what the family shared
- `articles/` – newspaper and sports articles that mention family members
- `records/` – county histories, court and death records, and Find a Grave transcriptions

Each file starts with its source link and the date it was retrieved. Obituaries and articles remain
the work of their publishers; they are kept here for family reference.
