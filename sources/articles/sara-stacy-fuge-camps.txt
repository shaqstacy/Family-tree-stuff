Sara Beth (Campbell) Stacy served on staff with Lifeway's FUGE Camps (Christian summer camps for
students in grades 6-12) at Shorter University (Rome, Ga.), the University of Mobile (Mobile, Ala.),
and Carson-Newman University (Jefferson City, Tenn.), under Will Snipes.

Will Snipes: a sixth-grade language arts teacher at Blue Ridge Middle School in Greer, S.C., and a former
Teacher of the Year there and for Greenville County Schools, who has served as a FUGE camp pastor and
director for more than 20 summers in the U.S. and abroad (Wales, Mexico, Bolivia, Ecuador, Canada).

Public FUGE pages list camp pastors and worship leaders but not other staff, so Sara's service could
not be confirmed from online records.
