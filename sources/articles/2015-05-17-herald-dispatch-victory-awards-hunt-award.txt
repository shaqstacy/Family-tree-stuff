Published: May 17, 2015 (69th Victory Awards Dinner, WesBanco Arena, Wheeling)
 WHEELING — Rakeem Cato's desire to play football at the professional level prevented the former Marshall University football player from attending the 69th Victory Awards Dinner on Sunday in WesBanco Arena.
 Spring Valley High School football player Trevor Stacy, a Marshall recruit, was the Hunt Award recipient as the state's prep lineman of the year.
 "It's an honor to win this," Stacy said. "I have so many people to thank who made this possible."
