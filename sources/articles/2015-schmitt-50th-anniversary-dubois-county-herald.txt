 50 years: William J. and Carolyn S. Schmitt 
 Dawn Mazur 
 Jul 23, 2015
 Jul 23, 2015 
 William J. “Bill” and Carolyn S. Schmitt of Jasper celebrated their 50th wedding anniversary with a Mass of thanksgiving at 5 p.m. Saturday, July 18, in Holy Family Catholic Church in Jasper.  A family dinner was held at KlubHaus 61 following the Mass.
 William Schmitt and Carolyn Zoglman were married July 24, 1965, in Holy Family Catholic Church. Father Joseph Anders officiated. Their attendants were Rosemary (Zoglman) Stratman, Elizabeth (Zoglman) Folz, Anna Lou Jerger, Rosie (Drach) Voegrel, John Schmitt, Jim Schmitt, Joe Zoglman, Ed Zoglman Jr. and the late Maria Stallings.    
 Mr. and Mrs. Schmitt are the parents of four sons, Jason Schmitt and wife Chrissy and Nathan Schmitt and wife Allison of Jasper, Todd Schmitt and wife Lynn of Schererville, and Luke Schmitt and wife Grace of Indianapolis. They have 10 grandchildren.
