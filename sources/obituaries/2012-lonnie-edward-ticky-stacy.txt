Source: Obituary text shared by Trevor Stacy (also on Find a Grave memorial 83371208)
Transcribed: November 7, 2026

Lonnie Edward "Ticky" Stacy, 65, of Oceana, WV passed away on Tuesday, January 10, 2012, at Raleigh General Hospital from injuries sustained in an automobile accident. He was a U.S. Navy veteran and a retired coal auger operator.

Lonnie was born July 14, 1946 at Hanover, WV, a son of the late Lonnie Stacy (1919-1981) and Hattie Margaret Riffe-Stacy (1925-2011).

Survivors include his loving wife of 45 years, Delma Jean Prichard-Stacy; two daughters, Angie Stacy-Stewart & husband Kevin Stewart of Daniels, WV, and Lori Stacy-Barlow & companion Eddie Pruitt of Oceana; a son, Ricky Stacy & wife Annette "Annie" Stacy of Lavalette, WV; four sisters, Sue Stacy-Egnor, Sharon Stacy-Lester, Kathy Stacy-Laney and Donna Stacy-Walker; two brothers, William "Larry" Stacy (1945-2016) and Randall Stacy; six grandchildren, Brandon Stacy, Laken Barlow, Alex Barlow, Trevor Stacy, Taylor Stewart and Chandler Stacy; Jeremy DeLong who was like a son; a special cousin Johnny "Smokie" Blankenship; and a host of other loving family and friends.

Funeral service will be held at the New Life Church, Jesse, WV with Rev. Jackie Lester officiating. Burial, with Military rites by the Wyoming County Veterans Honor Guard, in the Palm Memorial Gardens, Matheny, WV.
