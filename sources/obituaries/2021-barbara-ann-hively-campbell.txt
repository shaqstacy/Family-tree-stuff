BARBARA ANN CAMPBELL, 86, of Catlettsburg, Ky., went to be with the Lord on Friday, March 12, 2021 at Kings Daughter Medical Center. Funeral services will be held on Monday March 15, 2021 at 11 a.m. at the Altizer Baptist Church with Pastor Cledith V. Campbell Jr. officiating. Entombment will follow at Rose Hill Burial Park, Ashland, Ky. She was born on March 10, 1935 in Charleston, W.Va., the daughter of the late William and Helen Irene Turner Hively. In addition to her parents she was preceded in death by her husband, Cledith Vernon Campbell Sr., five sisters and one brother. She was a retired nurse from Kings Daughter Medical Center and a member of the Altizer Baptist Church. She is survived by her children: Cledith Jr. and Ruthie Campbell, Michael Ray Campbell, Paul Thomas and Sandra Kay Campbell, Robin Joy Campbell and Danny Lee and Margie Campbell; 11 grandchildren, 15 great grandchildren and one brother, Jerry Hively. Friends may call from 10 a.m. until service time at the church. Reger Funeral Home Ceredo-Kenova Chapel is assisting the family. In lieu of flowers contributions may be made to the Altizer Baptist Church, 4201 Altizer Ave, Huntington, WV 25705. Online condolences may be made to the family at www.regerfh.com.
