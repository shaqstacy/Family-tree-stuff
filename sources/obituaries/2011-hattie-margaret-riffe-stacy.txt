The life and times of Hattie Margaret Riffe Stacy, 85 of Justice, who went home to be with the Lord on Tuesday, January 4, 2011, at Williamson ARH in South Williamson, Kentucky.
Born on November 13, 1925, at Ikes Fork, to Larkin and Amanda Lee Riffe. She grew up on a farm in Wild Cat, Hanover. She was the last surviving child being preceded in death by her three brothers: Andrew, George, and Jesse Riffe and her four sisters: Etta Bailey, Violet Lester, Stella Lester, and Pearl Riffe.
At the age of seventeen she went to work at a restaurant in Iaeger and during World War II, she went to work at a shipyard in Brunswick Georgia. She later returned home and married Lonnie Stacy from Blackie (Hurley) Virginia. They resided in Justice for most of their married life. They were blessed with seven children.
Hattie spent her life doing what she loved most, taking care of her family and home. She enjoyed cooking, quilting, cross stitch, embroidery, and playing cards. The thing she enjoyed most was spending time with her wonderful family and friends, just hanging out, talking, laughing, reminiscing and a good long game of Rook or Spades. She was a wonderful mother who was always there to help in whatever the need. She was such a joy to be around and if you were down she would lift you up and brighten your day. Heaven is one star brighter now an angel on earth has gotten her wings. She was loved and will be greatly missed by everyone.
Those left to love and cherish her memory and to mourn her passing include her three sons: Larry Stacy (companion, Jennifer Rollyson) of Baisden, Lonnie "Ticky" (Delma) Stacy of Oceana, and Randall (Gladys) Stacy of Hanover; her four daughters: Sue (Roger) Egnor of Winfield, Sharon (Jackie) Lester of North Spring, Kathy (Eric) Laney of Van, and Donna (Keith) Walker of Oceana; her ten grandsons; her ten granddaughters; her eleven great-grandsons; her nineteen great-granddaughters, her 2 great-great-grandsons; her two great-great-granddaughters; and a host of other dear family and friends.
The family wishes to extend a special thank-you to: Gary and Illene Walls, Martha Kerns, Kay Aliff, Pam Harvey, Rhonda Sims, Jeff Pritchard, and Devonna West, for the kindness and care you gave our mother.
Friends may gather with the family at the Freedom Full Gospel Assembly House of Prayer at Hampden on Thursday, January 6th after 6 pm. A special service will be held at 7 pm.
Funeral services will be held at 1:00 pm on Friday, January 7th at the Freedom Full Gospel Assembly House of Prayer with the Rev. Jackie Lester and the Rev. Carl "Cab" Wolford officiating.
Burial will follow in the Riffe Cemetery at Right Fork of Hanover.
She will be carried to her final resting place by her grandsons.
Honorary pallbearers will be Carl Stacy and Odis Riffe.
