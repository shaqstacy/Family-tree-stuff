From the Kentucky Mesa back home to the Ohio River

The family of Trevor, Sara and Jeremiah Stacy. Their roots run back through the Stacys and Prichards of Wyoming County, West Virginia, the Campbells of the Tri-State, the Folz family of Evansville, and the Stallings and Zoglman families: English Catholics who reached Maryland, German farmers from Bavaria, and the Kentucky brothers who built a Catholic colony in a dugout near the Navajo Nation. Their lines meet in Theresa Lavina Stallings and Edward “Bud” Zoglman, married in 1938, and continue through their daughter Libby into the Folz family of St. Louis and Evansville.

    Sara’s heritage

    Sara’s four grandparent lines, traced as far back as the records go. The Hively line is the deepest: German Lutheran families from Württemberg, Baden and the Palatinate who came to Pennsylvania in the 1700s and moved down the valleys into Virginia and West Virginia.
